Architecture Project

An architecture project in interior design focuses on seamlessly integrating structural elements with interior aesthetics to create spaces that are both functional and visually compelling. It involves close collaboration between architects and interior designers to ensure spatial planning, lighting, materials, and finishes align with the building’s form and purpose. This holistic approach results in well-balanced environments that enhance usability, flow, and the overall experience of the space.
